Small print: Free for personal use. Want one for your business? That's what the button below is for.

## How it's made
No 3D files, no image textures. Every character is sculpted from math, rigged, and given a pose and a motion for each emotion. On slower phones it quietly lowers its own quality so it never stutters.

## How it works
1. Tell me about your brand: the vibe, the colors, the weird details that make it yours.
2. I sculpt your character in 3D code: 8 emotions and 2 moves, ready to be the face of your site or AI agent.
3. You get it in about a week: the HTML, transparent PNGs of every emotion, and an embed for your site.

## Why this exists
I'm Benjamin, a design engineer. This started as a side quest: I tried to make a Peruvian punk avatar in a big-tech app and got something sad. So I built my own.
Avatar Studio is a Silverdesk Lab project, part play and part proof. When the designer also writes the code, the vision doesn't get lost in the handoff. No templates, no dress-up menus. Characters built exactly the way I imagined them.
